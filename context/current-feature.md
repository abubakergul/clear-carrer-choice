Funnel analytics: self-hosted `Event` model (migration `20260616000000_add_event_analytics`) + fire-and-forget `track()` (`analytics.ts`) + client `trackClient` (sendBeacon); instruments `chat_started`, `wall_reached` (client), `signed_up`, `insight_generated`, `exploration_completed` (server); read at `/api/dev/funnel` (open in dev, `?key=ADMIN_SECRET` in prod). Consistent logo: a single `Logo`/`LogoMark` component (the three-overlapping-rings mark) replaces the chat's person icon, the bare-text result nav, and the `✦` star on sign-in/register, and the dashboard sidebar was DRY'd onto it. Requires `ADMIN_SECRET` env var in production for the funnel endpoint.
