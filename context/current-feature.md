- Subtext: "This helps you test what actually fits — before you waste years going the wrong direction."
- CTA: "Try it →" — bg-black, px-7 py-4, rounded-2xl, text-lg font-semibold, shadow-md, scale-105 on hover, scale-95 on click
- Microcopy: "Takes 3 minutes. No account. No pressure."
- Staggered fade-in animation on load (eyebrow 200ms, headline 300ms, subtext 400ms, CTA 500ms)
- Radial gradient background: white center → #f3f4f6 edges
- Content slightly above center (not flat centered)
- max-w-lg, tight stack spacing
